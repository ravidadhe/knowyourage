About KnowYourAge

KnowYourAge is a simple online tool designed to make age and birthday calculations quick, clear and easy to use.

🎂 Our goal

Give people a fast way to calculate their exact age without complicated calculations or account registration.

What is KnowYourAge?

KnowYourAge.in is an online age calculator that uses a person's date of birth to calculate their current age in years, months and days. The tool also provides additional information such as the next birthday, day of birth, zodiac sign and useful life statistics.

Why did we create it?

Age calculations can become confusing when months have different numbers of days or when someone wants to know their age in total days, hours, minutes or seconds. KnowYourAge was created to make these calculations simple and understandable.

Our approach

  • Keep the calculator simple and easy to understand.
  • Make it responsive for both desktop and mobile devices.
  • Present results clearly without unnecessary steps.
  • Continue improving the website with useful age and date tools.
